Privacy Policy

Am I Subscribed? Chrome Extension  ·  Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Am I Subscribed? is a browser extension that helps you identify which YouTube videos are from channels you follow. This policy explains what data the extension touches, where it stays, and what it never does.

Data collected and stored

The extension reads and locally caches one type of data:

  • Channel display names — the names of channels shown on your YouTube Subscriptions page (youtube.com/feed/channels). These names are captured as you scroll through that page and stored in chrome.storage.local on your own device.

No account information, watch history, video data, or personally identifiable information is ever read or stored.

Where your data goes

Nowhere. All data is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local). The extension makes no network requests of its own — it never contacts any external server, database, or third-party service.

How to delete your data

Click the extension icon in your toolbar and press Clear data. This immediately removes all cached channel names from your browser. You can also clear it via Chrome's built-in storage controls at chrome://settings/siteData.

Analytics and telemetry

None. The extension contains no analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry of any kind.

Third-party services

The extension interacts with YouTube (youtube.com) only in the sense that it reads the rendered HTML of pages you visit. It does not use the YouTube Data API, does not authenticate with Google, and does not send any data to YouTube or Google beyond normal browser page requests you initiate yourself.

The popup contains an optional link to Ko-fi for voluntary support. Clicking it opens Ko-fi's website in a new tab; the extension passes no data to Ko-fi.

Children's privacy

The extension does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date above will change. Substantive changes will be noted in the extension's Chrome Web Store changelog.
