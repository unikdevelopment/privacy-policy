Privacy Policy

Developer: Unik Development — unikdevelopment@gmail.com

Last updated: May 2026

YTFilter is a browser extension that filters YouTube video cards based on criteria you configure. This policy explains exactly what data the extension accesses and where it goes.

Data we collect

None. YTFilter does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. There is no backend server, no account system, and no analytics.

Data stored on your device

YTFilter stores two things locally in your browser:

  • Your filter settings — saved to chrome.storage.sync. This includes your slider positions, keyword lists, whitelist, and toggle states. If you are signed into Chrome/Edge, this data syncs across your own devices via Google's or Microsoft's sync infrastructure. It is never sent to YTFilter.
  • Subscriber count cache — saved to chrome.storage.local. When the subscriber filter is active, the extension fetches the YouTube page for each unique channel you encounter and stores the subscriber count locally for 12 hours to avoid repeat requests. This cache stays on your device only.

Network requests

When the "Min subscribers" filter is enabled, YTFilter makes fetch() requests to https://www.youtube.com/@channelname (the channel's own public page) to read the subscriber count. These requests:

  • Go directly to YouTube — not through any YTFilter server
  • Include your browser's YouTube cookies (so YouTube sees these as normal page loads from your account)
  • Are limited to 2 concurrent requests at a time
  • Are cached for 12 hours so the same channel is not fetched repeatedly

Separately, if you use the popup's "Report an issue" form, the message you type is sent directly to https://unikdev.app/api/extension-report so it reaches the developer — nothing else about your browser or activity is included in that request. Uninstalling the extension also opens a feedback page at unikdev.app; the extension name and version number are included in that page's URL so we know which version was uninstalled, but nothing further about you or your browser is sent unless you choose to fill out and submit that page's form.

No other network requests are made by this extension. All other filtering (view count, date, duration, keywords, ALL CAPS detection) is done by reading text already present in the YouTube page's DOM — no requests needed.

Permissions explained

  • storage — to save your filter settings and subscriber count cache locally
  • tabs — to send messages from the popup to the active YouTube tab when you change a filter, and to clear the toolbar badge count when you navigate away from YouTube
  • host permission: youtube.com — to inject the filtering script into YouTube pages and to fetch channel pages for subscriber counts
  • host permission: unikdev.app — used only if you submit the optional "Report an issue" form in the popup, so your message reaches the developer

Third parties

YTFilter shares no data with any third party. The only external services the extension communicates with are YouTube itself, which you are already using, and unikdev.app, which is only contacted if you choose to submit feedback.

Children

YTFilter does not knowingly collect information from anyone. It collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If the extension is updated in a way that changes data handling, this policy will be updated and the version date above will change.
