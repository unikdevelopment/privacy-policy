Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Overview

ScrollDive is a browser extension that toggles fullscreen mode based on scroll direction. Your privacy is important to us — the extension works entirely on your device with no data collection whatsoever.

Data storage

The extension stores the following settings locally on your device using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.local API:

  • Enabled/disabled state — whether the extension is active
  • Sensitivity preference — your chosen scroll sensitivity level (1–5)
  • Per-site mode flag — whether per-site filtering is turned on
  • Site allow list — hostnames you have explicitly added
  • Site exclude list — hostnames you have explicitly excluded
  • Preview mode flag — whether preview mode is enabled

All data is stored locally in your browser and never leaves your device.

Data collection

This extension does not:

  • Collect, transmit, or share any personal information
  • Track your browsing history or behavior
  • Send any data to external servers or third parties
  • Use analytics, telemetry, or advertising services
  • Access cookies, passwords, or form data

Permissions

Permission Why it's needed
storage Save your preferences locally
activeTab Read the current tab's URL to power per-site and exclude-list features
Content script on all URLs Detect scroll direction on every page so fullscreen can be triggered automatically

Third-party services

None. The extension has zero external dependencies and makes no network requests.
